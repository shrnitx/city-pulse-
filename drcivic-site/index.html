A better way to follow through

When updates are scattered across chats, calls and spreadsheets, it is hard to see the full picture. DRCivic brings the report, community signal, responsible team and status updates into one shared workflow.

One connected process

From “someone should fix this” to someone owns it.

DRCivic turns scattered complaints into visible, manageable cases. Each stage gives people more clarity about what was reported and what happens next.

01 / REPORT

Give the problem a place

Submit an issue with the details that matter: what happened, where it happened, and supporting evidence when needed.

1
02 / VERIFY

Make the signal clearer

Community confirmations and related reports help administrators understand recurring issues and review a more complete case.

2
03 / RESOLVE

Keep progress in view

Administrators verify, assign and update cases. Residents can follow the status instead of wondering whether anyone acted.

Tools for both sides of the process: a straightforward reporting experience for the community, and a structured work queue for the people responsible for responding.

01 / COMMUNITY

One shared picture

Reports, evidence, comments and confirmations sit with the issue they relate to — not in disconnected conversation threads.

02 / CASE MANAGEMENT

Ownership, not ambiguity

Review, verify, assign, escalate and update cases through a defined flow that makes the next step easier to see.

03 / INSIGHT

Patterns worth acting on

Analytics help teams review case volume, categories, status, resolution times and repeat problems across their community.
